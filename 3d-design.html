3D Design & Printing

What We Do

From Concept to Creation

Phoenix Conexus offers precision 3D design and printing services across four categories — Industrial, Modelling, Plan & Build, and Prototype & Print. Whether you need a technical industrial design, a detailed 3D model, a custom enclosure, or a finished printed product, we have the expertise to deliver.

Our 3D work spans a wide range of applications — from telecommunications infrastructure models and custom mounts, to creative models, functional parts, and bespoke accessories. Every project gets the same attention to detail that defines our consulting work.

If you can imagine it, we can help you build it.
