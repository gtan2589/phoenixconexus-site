About Phoenix Conexus

Our Story

Experience You Can Count On

Phoenix Conexus Inc. is a Design & Telecommunications Consulting firm based in Southern Ontario, serving clients across Canada and around the world. We operate across three divisions — Telecom, Architecture, and 3D Design & Printing — bringing the same standard of professional excellence to every engagement.

The person behind Phoenix Conexus has been working in telecommunications since 1990 — over 35 years of field and office experience that spans fibre optics, wireless, In-Building DAS, project management, and architectural design. That depth of expertise is what Phoenix Conexus was built on when we opened our doors in 2006.

We believe that to truly serve our clients, we must first understand their business. That philosophy has guided every project we've taken on for the past 20 years — and it's what keeps our clients coming back.
