What We Do

Field Experience Meets Office Precision

With roots in telecommunications going back to 1990, Phoenix Conexus brings over 35 years of hands-on field experience to every project. We understand what it takes to get real work done — and we know how to translate that back into the drawings, processes, and budgets managed in the office.

Our telecom division covers the full project lifecycle across five areas: Engineering, In-Building DAS, Planning, Project Management, and Survey. Clients have included major industry names — Ericsson, Primus, TeraGo, Galaxy Broadband, RYCOM, and more.

We deliver professional work on time and on budget. Every time.
